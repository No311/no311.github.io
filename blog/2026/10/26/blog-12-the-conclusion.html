The Conclusion

Oct 26, 2026

Well, here I am, with a notably different outlook from the last blog post.

It was Thursday, 15 October 2026, I realized somewhat jarringly that despite my optimistic planning and my pep talks to myself, the postdoc in Singapore was probably not going to work out in the way I hoped, and that I was better served to call it a day and try again in the Netherlands.

It is therefore official: by the time this is posted online, I will be back in the Netherlands. It is the end of this era. The decision was hard, but I think it is the right one.

I have arranged my departure and my lease. Meanwhile, I have met once again with some people that I’m definitely going to miss. Erfan, Martin, Sungmin, David, Yusuf, Nurjamal, Zhao Huan, Andrew and Kareem and Cheryl, I shall miss you. You’ve been great to hang out with; thank you for your company and the fun activities and times.

In light of this development, I spent most of last week and the current week with some of the aforementioned friends, to enjoy the remaining time I had here. It is funny how much time can go in a week when momentous things are happening, compared to how little time goes in a day when everything goes smoothly.

In the end, I couldn’t meet with David again before I left, for he will be away from Singapore until the 7th of November; thank you for our nature walk, and the good food of Holland Village. Let’s meet again later.

Thursday the 15th of October, when I was still coming to terms with the decision, I went to eat with Erfan. He introduced me to a nice bar near West Coast Plaza, which I have visited a few more times over the last weeks. They had the ingenious innovation of the IPA triple, and it was quite tasty.

One Tuesday ago, the 20th of October, I went to 99 Buona Vista Kitchen with Martin, Sungmin, Zhao Huan and Nurjamal, where we had a fun shared dinner and discussed multiple topics, like Dutch cuisine (I apologise, but it was fun) and future plans.

Then Thursday, I met up once again with Erfan, as we went to eat an Angus steak at the Yacht Club at the West Coast Park. Particularly noteworthy was that the haze had receded a little; the moon was actually visible, so the view from the Yacht Club was great. So was the conversation; me and Erfan discussed the differences between Singapore and the Netherlands, including the notion of gezelligheid. I am looking forward to seeing you in the Netherlands, Erfan, and thank you again for your company in Singapore.

Friday, I ate my last green curry at Sanook in the West Coast Plaza (which I quite enjoyed in my time here). I was joined by Sungmin, who has been a good friend to me here in Singapore as well. It turns out we both knew the bar I had gone to with Erfan, and discussing interesting ideas and a bit of the future, we had a nice Singaporean beer combined with an Oktoberfest beer. Thank you again, Sungmin, for the fun times we had here.

After the beer, I went back home to make an arretjescake, as on Saturday I joined a great barbeque organised by my neighbours. The food was delicious; it turns out I have become unused to eating a lot, but I gave it my best, and I met some cool people too.

Sunday, I went to eat with Martin one last time; we ate filipino food, which was delicious, and we explored Orchard and Plaza Singapura a little. Of the people I have to thank, I have to thank Martin especially, as without him I probably wouldn’t have seen half of the parts of Singapore that I have seen. I also quite enjoyed working with you, Martin, and I wish you good luck on the path to graduation.

Monday the 26th, I met with my agent, Rebecca, who has been a great help to me with regards to apartment matters. She agreed to take some stuff I couldn’t take with me off my hands so it did not have to be thrown away, which I’m very thankful for. We also discussed food and good ways of preventing stress; thanks for everything, Rebecca, and good luck with your business idea!

I then went to chill with Kareem, enjoying some beers and discussing music, vacations, games and Kareem’s start-up. Thanks again to you and Cheryl, Kareem, for being such good company (and for letting me pet your adorable pets). Please keep up the cool events too, these kinds of events are what brings a neighbourhood close together.

Tuesday, I prepared my suitcases and joined Yusuf once more for a delicious cola float. We discussed his trip to Germany, future plans and consultancy. Yusuf, thanks for being great company in our dinners together and I wish you luck with your plans!

On the way back from my dinner with Yusuf, I also met Nurjamal on the bus by chance; good luck with your PhD! I then said by to my neighbour in the room opposite to mine, Andrew, and went to the airport.

The flight went well, and though I have a bit of jetlag, it mostly means my current sleep schedule is healthier than it has been in years. I am happy to be back in the Netherlands, and so is Poekie, who was very happy to see me again.

So what is next? I don’t know. I think that after my truly hectic and exciting year (finishing thesis, defending thesis, searching for a job, arranging move to Singapore, postdoc in Singapore, returning from Singapore), I can allow letting myself properly rest for a bit, and enjoy things like this tree for a moment:

Tom Out.

As this blog ranges over quite some time, I have read a few books, played a few games and seen a few movies, including The Bridge (Bron/Broen) series 1, Welcome to the Sticks, Steamworld Dig 1 and 2, and The Sheep Detectives. I would like to specifically recommend Lil Gator Game, another great recommendation by Joep. Very relaxing and very calming, and exactly what I needed after Decision Thursday.

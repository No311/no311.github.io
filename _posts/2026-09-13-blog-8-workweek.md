This is going to be a short blog post; this past week has been filled with work

On Wednesday, I had a meeting in which some expectation mismatches were cleared and we decided on the project I would be doing. I am happy to say that it's an interesting project that makes good use of some of my existing skills but also allows me to get some experience in things I have not done before.
To get it off the ground, and in preparation for next week, I spent Saturday working as well (which was fairly relaxing).

Other than work, I went to the Sushi Tei in Holland village on Wednesday with David; one restaurant I will return to.

Well, here I am, with a notably different outlook from the last blog post.

It was Thursday, 15 October 2026, I realized somewhat jarringly that despite my optimistic planning and my pep talks to myself, the postdoc in Singapore was probably not going to work out in the way I hoped, and that I was better served to call it a day and try again in the Netherlands.

It is therefore official: by the time this is posted online, I will be back in the Netherlands. It is the end of this era. The decision was hard, but I think it is the right one.

I have arranged my departure and my lease. Meanwhile, I have met once again with some people that I'm definitely going to miss. Erfan, Martin, Sungmin, David, Yusuf, Nurjamal, Zhao Huan, Andrew and Kareem and Cheryl, I shall miss you. You've been great to hang out with; thank you for your company and the fun activities and times.

In light of this development, I spent most of last week and the current week with some of the aforementioned friends, to enjoy the remaining time I had here. It is funny how much time can go in a week when momentous things are happening, compared to how little time goes in a day when everything goes smoothly.

In the end, I couldn't meet with David again before I left, for he will be away from Singapore until the 7th of November; thank you for our nature walk, and the good food of Holland Village. Let's meet again later.

Thursday the 15th of October, when I was still coming to terms with the decision, I went to eat with Erfan. He introduced me to a nice bar near West Coast Plaza, which I have visited a few more times over the last weeks. They had the ingenious innovation of the IPA triple, and it was quite tasty.

One Tuesday ago, the 20th of October, I went to 99 Buona Vista Kitchen with Martin, Sungmin, Zhao Huan and Nurjamal, where we had a fun shared dinner and discussed multiple topics, like Dutch cuisine (I apologise, but it was fun) and future plans.

Another short week. I've been working hard on my project.
This week, I focused on the background theory, and I learnt many cool things about Incorrectness (Separation) Logic and its proofs.
In short, it turns out that where we can over-approximate the effects of programs to guarantee correctness, we can also under-approximate the effects to find proof of bugs. This has been utilized in automated reasoning to great success.
This is probably not news to some of you, and though it was to me, the principles made sense.

Tuesday, other than work, I went to eat with Sungmin and Martin at Nana Thai, whose Green Curry I had wanted to try for a while already. What I did not know until I had ordered it, however, is that the curry was made for more than one person to eat. I gave it my best, but I didn't finish it in the end. It was delicious, however.

On Wednesday, me, Sungmin, Martin and Bernice went to the final movie of the Dollars trilogy, The Good, the Bad and the Ugly. Even though I went in already knowing most things that I was going to see, I was still quite impressed by how much more ambitious the movie was in scope and character development. Tuco is a delight to watch and an excellent protagonist, even if he is not the hero, and the way the war impedes upon the world of these cowboys and bounty hunters is also quite moving. An absolute recommendation from me.
